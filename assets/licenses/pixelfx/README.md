# James shield bash impact

The sprite sheet adapts [PixelFX Shield Block](https://github.com/patch-gg/pixelfx/blob/5294518ce7c72e48b6100a744596e0acf57f2826/src/fx/impacts/shield-block.js), distributed under the included MIT license. The upstream effect adapts [Hugo Duprez's Sprite Fusion shield](https://www.spritefusion.com/pixel-art-vfx/shield), copyright 2026 Hugo Duprez, MIT.

The transparent sheet was exported at 20 fps with a teal and gold palette, then reduced to half resolution with nearest-neighbor sampling for Roti & Benteng.

## Golem Es

The Frost Nova and freeze/shatter sheets adapt [Frost nova](https://github.com/patch-gg/pixelfx/blob/5294518ce7c72e48b6100a744596e0acf57f2826/src/fx/ice/frost-nova.js) and [Freeze](https://github.com/patch-gg/pixelfx/blob/5294518ce7c72e48b6100a744596e0acf57f2826/src/fx/ice/freeze.js) under the included MIT license. The Nova uses only the forward half of the effect. Freeze omits the stock character so the ice can overlay any hero. Both exports are retimed for the game. The freeze fracture and shards adapt [Hugo Duprez's Sprite Fusion ice effect](https://www.spritefusion.com/pixel-art-vfx/ice), copyright 2026 Hugo Duprez, MIT.

## Efek kekalahan entitas biasa

- `assets/sprites/effects/enemy-defeat-poof.png` adapts [PixelFX Poof](https://github.com/patch-gg/pixelfx/blob/main/src/fx/spawn/poof.js) (MIT License), based on Hugo Duprez's Sprite Fusion explosion puffs (MIT License, Copyright 2026 Hugo Duprez), exported with a purple-slate palette at 20 fps and half resolution.
- `assets/sprites/effects/hero-defeat-soul.png` adapts [PixelFX Soul rise](https://github.com/patch-gg/pixelfx/blob/main/src/fx/spawn/soul-rise.js) (MIT License), based on Hugo Duprez's Sprite Fusion movement/heal effects (MIT License, Copyright 2026 Hugo Duprez), omitting the ground shadow overlay and exported at half resolution.
